Not Sure Where to Start?

All of our classes are suitable for every experience level, from complete beginners to experienced Pilates enthusiasts. Explore each class below to find the right fit for your goals.

New to Pilates? Beginner classes focus on learning the fundamentals, building confidence, and developing proper technique in a supportive environment. Our instructors will guide you through each movement at a comfortable pace.
We recommend completing a minimum of 5 beginner classes before progressing to higher levels.

Suitable for those with previous Pilates experience who are confident with the basics. Expect more challenging exercises, increased intensity, and opportunities to further develop strength, balance, and control.

Open to everyone, regardless of experience. Exercises can be adapted to suit your individual ability, allowing both beginners and experienced participants to benefit from the same class.

Our Advanced classes offer an elevated, high-intensity Pilates experience designed for confident, experienced clients. These sessions move at a stronger pace and incorporate complex combinations, deeper stability work, and advanced progressions. You'll be guided through powerful, athletic sequences that demand control, endurance, and focus, creating a rewarding full-body workout that leaves you feeling strong, grounded, and accomplished.
