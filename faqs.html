Our Classes

We offer a range of classes that include, reformer pilates, mat pilates, jumpboard, and strength pilates. These classes are designed to build strength, improve mobility and help you feel your best.

We're unable to accept walk-ins, all classes must be booked online in advance. Same day bookings are welcome, with the latest booking available up to 15 minutes before the session start time.

Bookings & Cancellations

Head to our Schedule to view classes and make a booking request. We will get back to you shortly.

You can cancel without incurring any charge or loss of credit if done prior than 24 hours of the booked session. We understand that things can come up, and we kindly ask that you give us at least 24 hours' notice if you're unable to attend.
Cancellations made after and within 24 hours of the class start time, will be charged as follows:
  • Class Pass holders will lose one class credit
  • Members on unlimited plans will be charged a £17 no-show-fee

Studio Essentials

Wear comfortable, form-fitting activewear that allows you to move freely. This is a ladies only studio so we want you to be as comfortable as possible. Grip socks are a must for safety and hygiene and if you don't have any, they can be purchased in the studio if needed.
